Evaluating Polynomials Fast

You may have heard about Horner's method that allows evaluating a polynomial of degree n in n multiplications. But did you know that with a bit of preprocessing of the coefficients, often n/2 multiplications suffice? You can use this to approximate functions like exp, sin, cos, or to evaluate polynomials in cryptography, hashing, and coding theory. Simple type a polynomial below, pick your field, and we'll preprocess it for you.
