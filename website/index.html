Half-the-multiplications polynomial chains

Type a polynomial and get back a straight-line evaluation chain, as mathematics, as C, or as a graph. This paper is the schedule of Fast Evaluation of Polynomials with Rational Preprocessing (preprint, 2026; arXiv link to follow): a monic polynomial of degree n in ⌊n/2⌋+1 multiplications after an everywhere-defined rational preprocessing of its coefficients (a non-monic input costs one more scalar multiplication, by the leading coefficient), shown next to Horner, Estrin, Rabin–Winograd, Knuth–Eve, and Pan on the same input. Three field settings: exact rationals (ℚ, or ℝ with the same exact chain shown in doubles), Mersenne-prime fields as used in polynomial hashing, and binary fields GF(2k) for carry-less hashing.

All arithmetic is exact (BigInt rationals, Mersenne-prime fields GF(2k−1), carryless binary fields GF(2k)). The ℝ setting runs the same exact rational preprocessing as ℚ and then shows and emits the chain's constants as doubles; that rounding alone is why it is reported as ≈ numeric. The generated C matches the code used for the paper's experiments (PCLMULQDQ / PMULL carryless multiplication, 128-bit Mersenne reduction).
